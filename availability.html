How this works

Pick your name, then tap the days you can make. Tap again to say you can’t, and a third time to clear it back to no answer — “can’t make it” and “haven’t said” are different things, and only one of them is a reason to chase somebody.

A day goes green when every one of you can make it, amber when one can’t, and red when two or more can’t. Days nobody has ruled out but not everyone has confirmed stay grey — the count on each day says how many are in so far.

Hover or tap-and-hold a day to see exactly who. Your own answers show a white outline, and nothing is saved until you press the button at the bottom.
