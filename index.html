How to vote

Go through the list and mark each song MUST (fight for it), YES (happy to play it), or NO (leave it out). Anything you skip counts as neutral.

Use MUST sparingly — about 5 songs. It's a tie-breaker, not a second yes.

When you're done, put your name at the top and hit Copy my votes, then paste the text into the band chat — or enter your votes straight into the shared Google Sheet, which tallies itself.
