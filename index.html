How to vote

Pick your name, then mark each song MUST (fight for it), YES (happy to play it), MAYBE (could live with it) or NO (leave it out). Anything you skip counts as neutral.

The set is built purely on total score — the highest-scoring songs go in until 90 minutes is full. MAYBE still helps a song get in, it just helps less than a YES.

Use MUST sparingly — about 5 songs. It's a tie-breaker, not a second yes.

Hit Save my votes at the bottom and they go straight into the shared sheet. Come back and change them whenever you like. See how it's going on the results page.
