How this works

Pick your name, then mark each song Not started, In progress or Know it. Anything you leave alone stays blank — blank means “haven’t said”, which is not the same as not started.

This is separate from the vote. Marking a song as known does not put it in the set, and voting MUST on a song you have never played is fine — that is the point of having both.

The dots on each row are the whole band: filled = knows it, ringed = learning it, empty = not started or hasn’t said.
